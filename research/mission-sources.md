## 5. Research Guidelines

For every mission, document:

1. Mission purpose and historical context.
2. Launch date and destination.
3. Spacecraft and instruments involved.
4. Major scientific discoveries.
5. Mission ending and current hardware status.
6. Physical location or final resting place, where known.
7. Long-term scientific and historical legacy.
8. Official NASA sources and image credits.

## 6. Accuracy Rules

- Distinguish inactive hardware from spacecraft that are still operational.
- Do not describe every object as abandoned or destroyed.
- Separate spacecraft status from individual instrument status.
- Do not invent distances, telemetry, mission dates, or discoveries.
- Label artistic animations and illustrative visualisations clearly.
- Credit all images and respect source usage terms.

**Research principle:** Every mission story must be engaging, scientifically accurate, and supported by credible sources.
