# THE SILENT FRONTIER
## Mission Story Planning
Team: STARSTUFF

### Project Vision
An immersive, cinematic digital archive exploring humanity's machines left across the Solar System and beyond. Each exhibit tells the story of a spacecraft, its mission, discoveries, and lasting legacy.

### Target Audience
All ages — students, educators, astronomy enthusiasts, and casual space explorers.

### Experience Style
Hybrid interactive experience combining:
- Cinematic storytelling
- Interactive mission exploration
- Scientific data visualisations
- Educational mission timelines
- Original ambient sound design

### Main Website Sections

1. HOME
   - Cinematic introduction
   - Project overview
   - Featured mission
   - Explore button

2. EXPLORER ATLAS
   - Moon
   - Mars
   - Asteroid belt
   - Deep space
   - Interactive spacecraft markers

3. MISSION STORIES
   Each mission includes:
   - Beginning
   - Launch
   - Journey
   - Scientific discoveries
   - Mission ending and current status
   - Legacy

4. VOYAGER EXPERIENCE
   - Dedicated Voyager 1 experience
   - Journey timeline
   - Planetary encounters
   - Golden Record
   - Heliopause and interstellar exploration
   - Scientific legacy

5. MISSION INTELLIGENCE
   - Mission duration comparisons
   - Destination distribution
   - Mission milestones
   - Hardware status
   - Scientific discovery statistics

6. HUMAN CONNECTION
   - The people behind the missions
   - The Golden Record
   - Humanity's messages to the universe

### Initial Mission Shortlist

1. Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments (ALSEP)
2. Surveyor 3
3. Sojourner / Mars Pathfinder
4. Opportunity
5. InSight
6. Dawn
7. Pioneer 10
8. Voyager 1 (featured mission)

Additional missions can be introduced as development progresses.
